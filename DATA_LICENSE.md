The MesaSays restaurant dataset in [`data/`](data/) (places, contact details, hours, booking,
map positions, menus and prices) is published under the **Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**,
the same license as OpenStreetMap: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
(full text in `data/LICENSE-ODbL.txt`).

You are free to copy, reuse and build on it, as long as you:
- credit it: "© MesaSays contributors (mesasays.com), ODbL. Map positions © OpenStreetMap contributors.";
- share any improved version of the database under the same license.

## What is in it, and what is not

Only facts from the restaurants' own websites, menus, booking pages and Instagram accounts,
from OpenStreetMap, or from MesaSays' own verification of the official site. The rest of what
mesasays.com shows (for example some addresses or map positions first collected from Google)
is left out until we confirm it from a source we can share. `data/report.json` lists, field by
field, how much was kept and how much was left out.

Map positions are computed from the address with OpenStreetMap Nominatim, so they are
© OpenStreetMap contributors, also under ODbL.
