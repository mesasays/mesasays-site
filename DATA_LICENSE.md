# Data license

The MesaSays restaurant dataset (places, menus, prices) will be published under the
**Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**, the same license as OpenStreetMap:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

You will be free to copy, reuse and build on it, as long as you credit MesaSays and share
your improvements under the same license.

**Status (2026-10-31): the data is not in this repository yet.** Part of the current
dataset was collected from sources we are not allowed to redistribute (for example Google).
We are separating it, field by field, so that only data from restaurants' own websites and
menus, OpenStreetMap, and our own visits is published. It will be released as a separate,
downloadable file.

Community posts (Say) belong to their authors and will be published under
**CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
